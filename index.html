About Coach Daren

Singapore's trusted golf professional
with a champion's perspective.

7.4K+
Instagram

Daren Lim is a certified PGA Golf Professional coaching at Tanah Merah Country Club (TMCC) — one of Singapore's most prestigious clubs. Recognised as one of the city's rising young coaches, he has built a trusted name in the local golf community.

With 31+ years of playing experience, 20 years of competitive tournament play, and 10+ years coaching, Daren brings a rare dual perspective: he knows what it takes to win, and he knows how to pass that on clearly. He has won multiple Club Championships, regional tournaments, and the National Championship.

His approach centres on understanding each student's lifestyle, goals, and personality — then building a plan that works for them specifically. He coaches beginners, juniors, weekend warriors, busy executives, and competitive amateurs with equal passion.
