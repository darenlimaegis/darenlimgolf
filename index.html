The Growing Need for Advanced Fire Safety

Large-scale fires from electrical faults and battery defects are increasing globally. ESS, EV infrastructure and data centres represent the fastest-growing fire risk and the largest emerging market for advanced suppression solutions.

Core Technology

Microcapsule Fire Suppression — A Decade of Innovation

GFI's proprietary nano-process microcapsule technology forms the foundation of every AEGIS product. Microscopic capsules rupture precisely at fire threshold temperatures, releasing extinguishing agents instantly at the point of ignition.

Unlike conventional gas or water systems, GFI's modular approach integrates directly into battery cells, cable trays and ESS modules — providing protection where traditional systems cannot reach.
